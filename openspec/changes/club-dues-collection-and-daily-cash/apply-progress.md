## Approved PR8 Agreement-Term Policy

- Every agreement requires a native `obligationId` owned by the socio.
- The agreed amount is an integer number of cents from 1 through the obligation's outstanding debt at creation or revision.
- Terms contain 1–60 installments; each installment has an integer cent amount and an ISO date-only `YYYY-MM-DD` due date.
- Due dates are valid, unique, strictly increasing, and not earlier than the agreement date; installment amounts sum exactly to the agreed amount.
- Rescheduling atomically supersedes the active agreement and creates one successor while preserving obligation identity, append-only debt facts, and allocation history.
